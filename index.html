THE EXPERIENCE
NOT JUST A PLAY. AN ENTIRE WORLD.

Presented by Starlight Anthem, ODYSSEY is a ground-breaking theatrical production blurring the boundary between cinematic narrative, classical composition, contemporary sonic culture, and live performance. A visceral voyage conceived to transport audiences into an uncharted dimension of storytelling.

NARRATIVE WORLD

STORY.

"ACROSS UNTAMED SEAS TO THE ETHIOPIAN HIGHLANDS, FLASHBACKS OF TRAUMA AND VISIONS OF SPACE COLLIDE."

CHAPTER 01

THE ESCAPE.

Takhar flees a violent encounter, abandoning the remnants of his former life to embark on an uncharted crossing over tempestuous oceanic depths.

CHAPTER 02

THE JOURNEY.

A grueling passage across midnight waters, where external storms mirror the turbulent tides of human memory, endurance, and sheer survival.

CHAPTER 03

THE VILLAGE.

Sanctuary discovered in a remote settlement within the Ethiopian highlands. Amid ancient soils, unexpected romance blooms beneath towering mountains.

CHAPTER 04

THE VISIONS.

Reality splinters as unsettling flashbacks and transcendent cosmic visions of outer space penetrate Takhar’s waking consciousness.

CHAPTER 05

THE RETURN.

A profound reckoning with destiny, history, and spiritual homecoming. The Odyssey culminates in an unforgettable convergence of light and revelation.

Composed by Donovan Fisher, ODYSSEY unites the delicate intimacy of solo grand piano with monumental electronic synthesizers and cinematic orchestration. The score bridges centuries of sonic tradition—channeling the emotional majesty of classical titans while pulsating with modern rhythms.

INNOVATIVE STAGECRAFT

SEE THE STORY
COME ALIVE.

ODYSSEY reconstructs the theatrical canvas. Utilizing architectural LED technology and multi-surface projection mapping, the stage transforms in real time from midnight oceanic storms into the sun-drenched Ethiopian highlands and celestial cosmic expanses.

01 // DISPLAY

MONUMENTAL LED SCREENS

Ultra-high-definition backdrop canvases delivering deep cinematic blacks, blinding bursts of celestial light, and hyper-realistic depth of field.

02 // IMMERSION

PROJECTION MAPPING

Multi-dimensional projection casting shifting textures across physical sets and stage architecture, blurring physical barriers and virtual worlds.

03 // ATMOSPHERE

SCULPTED THEATRICAL LIGHT

Synchronized atmospheric lighting, theatrical haze, and deep Odyssey crimson hues working in harmonic counterpoint with live musical cues.

AUDIENCE ADVISORY & VENUE POLICIES

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE.

ODYSSEY is recommended for patrons aged 12 and above. Children under 12 may only attend under direct adult supervision.

This production utilizes rapid strobe lighting, sudden bursts of darkness, high-intensity projection mapping, and theatrical smoke/haze effects throughout the performance.

The performance contains coarse language, depiction of violent encounters, psychological trauma themes, and mature thematic elements.

To preserve the uninterrupted continuity of the live experience, late arrivals will not be admitted once Act I begins. Latecomers may only take their seats during the 18-minute intermission at 8:10 PM.

Cameras, audio recorders, video capture devices, and unauthorized photography are strictly prohibited inside the auditorium. Mobile devices must remain silenced.

The Regal Theatre accommodates wheelchair accessibility and assistive hearing loops. For dedicated accessible seating arrangements, please contact the Ticketek accessible booking hotline directly.
